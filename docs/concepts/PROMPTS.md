## desktop-explore

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished high-fidelity AFC – Arena for Creatives application screen, a practical shippable interface. Shared visual system: charcoal background, slightly lighter panels, off-white clean sans-serif typography, restrained warm orange primary actions, thin borders, generous spacing, rounded 12px cards. Artworks provide rich color. Exact wordmark "AFC" and subtitle "Arena for Creatives". Show creator identities and vote counts. No blind voting, judging scores, verification codes, additional awards, chat, or generic social posts. One vote per account per entry costs 1 point, no repeat votes. Submission and voting share the same active period. All example artwork and people fictional. Crisp readable short labels, no watermark, no device mockup, no perspective, fill canvas with UI.
Landscape desktop screen 1536x1024 composition. Persistent left sidebar about 220px wide: "Explore", "Contests", "Submit", "Saved", "Wallet", "Notifications", "Profile"; bottom sidebar Settings and user avatar. Main header search and notification bell. NO bottom navigation.
Explore screen. Heading "Discover the arena". Featured active contest "Myth & Magic", cash prize "₱10,000", countdown "3d 12h left", button "View contest". Filters "All", "Art", "Cosplay", "New entries", "Most voted". Artwork cards with fantasy illustrations and adult cosplayers, creator names, contest labels, visible vote totals and bookmark icons. Mix art and cosplay. Wallet chip "100 points".
